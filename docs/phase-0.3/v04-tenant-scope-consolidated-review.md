# V04.3 Tenant Scope Consolidated Review

**Review type:** Consolidated static design and repository evidence review.  
**Decision baseline:** D1–D15 are `OWNER_APPROVED` (2026-11-06), as recorded in V04.2.  
**Gate result:** `V04 BLOCKED`; V05–V10 and Phase 1 remain `BLOCKED`.  
**Readiness conclusion:** The approved parts of the design may proceed to staged technical validation. This review does not establish implementation readiness, V04 PASS, or technical compatibility.

## 1. Executive summary

The V04.2 design is consistent with the approved boundaries when D1–D15 remain scoped to their stated policies. In particular, D9–D13 approve only P05 X01–X05 respectively, and D15 is a distinct Service Principal policy. Those approvals do not establish application authorization or a complete tenant-isolation boundary.

**Historical source snapshot at HEAD `cd183e4`:** `prisma/schema.prisma` contained the Organization, Company, Plant, OrganizationMembership, CompanyMembership and PlantAssignment models and the approved X01–X05 Prisma relations. Migration `20261107112700_v0451_tenant_scope_schema` (migration 7 of 9) contained the corresponding FK DDL and `RESTRICT`/`RESTRICT` actions. That source inspection did not verify a database. Separately, the V04.5.1 record dated 2026-11-08 documents a successful fresh replay of all nine checked-in migrations in a local disposable PostgreSQL environment; Prisma reported the database up to date, and a catalog audit validated all 19 FKs across 10 schemas and 18 tables (55 indexes and 9 migration records were counted). This later replay supersedes the earlier statement that a nine-migration fresh replay was not established, but it does not prove hosted/production state, a nine-migration staged upgrade, complete edge-level behavior, or tenant authorization. Provisioning workflows and end-to-end tenant authorization remain separate from schema/migration presence; the inspected source had no Organization/Company/Plant creation flow, while scoped-access service code created CompanyMembership and PlantAssignment records with transactional parent checks.

The older P05 matrix and ERD preserve historical X04/X05 candidate tuples. Later D9–D13 decisions are operative and their exact tuples remain unchanged in the overlays. This is documentation reconciliation, not a decision change. Invitation edges and P05 edges outside X01–X05 remain unapproved unless separately decided.

**Recommendation:** Keep V04 `BLOCKED`; verify the current database and complete the remaining case-level technical evidence before claiming validation. Do not create a duplicate migration: current source already contains X01–X05. Do not declare implementation-ready until actual scoped operations, authorization and adversarial evidence exist. Before implementing affected features, obtain any still-required owner decisions for invitation edges, a permission/grant catalog, unspecified lifecycle/status rules, and additional in-scope P05 edges.

## 5. Cross-document consistency and relationship findings

### 5.1 P05 approval and tuple history — reconciled

The original P05 matrix and physical ERD retain the earlier X04/X05 candidate tuples as historical records. Those tuples differ from the later D12/D13 approvals and must not be treated as current mappings. The current P05 overlay and ERD now identify the approved X01–X05 policies and point to the checked-in source implementation without rewriting candidate history. D2 invitation semantics do not approve Invitation edges; D15 does not approve additional edges; X07–X16 remain individual candidate decisions except for separately recorded approvals such as X06.

### 5.2 Entity and ownership coverage — HIGH

V04.2 defines conceptual Organization, Company, Plant, membership/assignment, scoped role, Role Definition, and Invitation concepts. These are proposals, not a finalized physical catalog. P07 requires a version-controlled entity registry containing owner, aggregate, lifecycle, identity, scope, relationships, authorization, contracts, and status; its validation backlog marks registry completeness and aggregate mapping as pending. Reconcile V04.2's proposed entities and relations with that registry, ownership boundaries, and ERDs before implementation. D15 is an approved policy but Service Principal and credential entities are not shown in the proposed ERD or candidate Prisma table; this omission is an allowable deferral only while the relevant background/integration work is out of scope.

### 5.3 Authorization and access boundary — BLOCKER for V04 PASS

P04 and P10 consistently separate authentication/session validity from business authorization. Yet no protected business operation currently demonstrates canonical-principal resolution, current membership and typed scope checks, action/target authorization, owner rules, or scope-constrained reads/writes. RLS is not evidenced and is not an implicit requirement or approved universal boundary. If selected later, it remains defense-in-depth, requires explicit table/policy decisions and least-privilege database roles, and depends on V07 proof of transaction-local context and pooled-connection isolation.

### 5.4 Unresolved owner decisions and implementation parameters — HIGH

The approved decisions do not supply: a complete action/resource/field/export permission catalog and grant matrix; lifecycle details outside the D1–D6 decisions; detailed invitation-edge choices; or P05 edge choices for additional business entities brought into scope. These are not silently resolved by the ERD, by D15, or by the fact that a proposed FK exists. Owner decisions must precede the affected implementation. P10 propagation bounds/cache TTLs and other session parameters remain values to select and test; they affect demonstrated authorization freshness but do not reverse the approved session architecture.

### 5.5 P09 documentation versus current manifest — MEDIUM

P09 closure and compatibility backlog prose still states exact runtime/package-manager versions are unselected, while the current manifest, lockfile, and `.nvmrc` contain concrete values. The validation register already records some V01/V03 evidence, but this documentation mismatch can cause an incorrect assumption about what is pinned or certified. Reconcile the P09 text and evidence references separately; a manifest pin is not proof that a new tenant compound relation, partial unique index, fallback, or authorization path works.

This sequence is a recommendation only. It does not update the execution register, authorize broad implementation, or change any decision.

3. **Close structural evidence gaps.** Existing historical disposable-database runs cover selected positive/negative cases, Company parent-key update restrictions, and selected D5/D1/D6 behavior.
