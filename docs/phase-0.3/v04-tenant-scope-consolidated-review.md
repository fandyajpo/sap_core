# V04.3 Tenant Scope Consolidated Review

**Review type:** Consolidated static design and repository evidence review.  
**Decision baseline:** D1–D15 are `OWNER_APPROVED` (2026-11-06), as recorded in V04.2.  
**Gate result:** `V04 BLOCKED`; V05–V10 and Phase 1 remain `BLOCKED`.  
**Readiness conclusion:** The approved parts of the design may proceed to staged technical validation. This review does not establish implementation readiness, V04 PASS, or technical compatibility.

## 1. Executive summary

The V04.2 design is internally consistent with the approved scope policies when its boundaries are preserved: canonical Better Auth identity is separate from membership and authorization; organization, company, and plant access are distinct; D1–D6 define specific membership/cardinality/history policies; D7–D8 and D14 define role-scope and ownership boundaries; D9–D13 approve only P05 X01–X05 respectively; and D15 approves a distinct, explicitly owned/scoped, non-impersonating Service Principal policy. Those approvals are policy decisions, not schema or enforcement evidence.

The repository does not yet implement this tenant model. `prisma/schema.prisma` contains two validation-fixture models and the four Better Auth persistence models only. The six-migration history contains no Organization, Company, Plant, membership, assignment, role, permission, invitation, Service Principal, tenant business-resource, RLS, or tenant-authorization structure. The inspected application has authentication configuration and a shared Prisma client, but no protected tenant business operation or tenant-scoped authorization/data-access path. No tenant isolation tests are present in the inspected source/test surfaces.

A cross-document P05 drift is material: the older P05 matrix still calls X01–X05 not individually approved and lists earlier candidate X04/X05 tuples. V04.2 records later, edge-specific approvals D9–D13 with different, explicit tuples. Treat the later D9–D13 approvals as the current owner decisions; reconcile the stale documentation without reopening or broadening those approvals. Invitation edges and P05 edges outside X01–X05 remain unapproved unless separately decided.

**Recommendation:** Begin a staged technical-validation workstream for approved D1–D15 policy and the approved X01–X05 relationship rules, testing each physical mapping or approved same-transaction fallback against the actual pinned PostgreSQL/Prisma/provider setup. Do not declare implementation-ready or change V04 from `BLOCKED` until actual scoped operations, authorization and adversarial evidence exist. Before implementing affected features, obtain the still-required owner decisions for invitation edges, the permission/grant catalog, any unspecified lifecycle/status rules, and any additional in-scope P05 edges.

## 4. D1–D15 policy consistency matrix

| **D1 — Organization slot** | `ACTIVE` and `SUSPENDED` occupy the one organization slot; `PENDING`, `REVOKED`, and `ENDED` do not. Pending/suspended/terminal rows do not grant access; reassignment ends the prior slot occupant first; preserve history and prevent concurrent duplicate occupants. | Consistent with the initial P03 operating policy. | **D3 — Suspension/revocation/regrant history** | Restore SUSPENDED on the same record; REVOKED/ENDED are terminal; later grants create new episodes; retain descendant history and make descendants ineffective when their parent membership is ineffective; restoring membership does not restore revoked sessions. | Consistent with D1 and P10 boundaries. ## 5. Cross-document consistency and relationship findings

### 5.1 P05 approval and tuple drift — HIGH

`docs/phase-0.1/05-cross-schema-relationship-policy.md` still says only X06 is individually approved and X01–X05 are not individually owner-approved. Its X04 candidate references `(user_id, organization_id)` to the Organization Membership and `(organization_id, company_id)` to Company; its X05 candidates use `(user_id, company_id)` to Company Membership and `(company_id, plant_id)` to Plant. The older physical ERD in `docs/phase-0.1/09-global-physical-erd.md` repeats those earlier candidate tuples.

V04.2 later records D9–D13 as owner approvals for X01–X05, with D12 and D13 explicitly specified tuples in the matrix above. This is documentation drift, not a reason to reopen or dilute the later approvals. The source records should be reconciled in a separate documentation change, preserving D9–D13 exactly. D2 invitation semantics do not approve Invitation edges; D15 does not approve additional edges; X07–X16 remain individual candidate decisions except for any separately recorded approval such as X06.

### 5.2 Entity and ownership coverage — HIGH

V04.2 defines conceptual Organization, Company, Plant, membership/assignment, scoped role, Role Definition, and Invitation concepts. These are proposals, not a finalized physical catalog. P07 requires a version-controlled entity registry containing owner, aggregate, lifecycle, identity, scope, relationships, authorization, contracts, and status; its validation backlog marks registry completeness and aggregate mapping as pending. Reconcile V04.2's proposed entities and relations with that registry, ownership boundaries, and ERDs before implementation. D15 is an approved policy but Service Principal and credential entities are not shown in the proposed ERD or candidate Prisma table; this omission is an allowable deferral only while the relevant background/integration work is out of scope.

### 5.3 Authorization and access boundary — BLOCKER for V04 PASS

P04 and P10 consistently separate authentication/session validity from business authorization. Yet no protected business operation currently demonstrates canonical-principal resolution, current membership and typed scope checks, action/target authorization, owner rules, or scope-constrained reads/writes. RLS is not evidenced and is not an implicit requirement or approved universal boundary. If selected later, it remains defense-in-depth, requires explicit table/policy decisions and least-privilege database roles, and depends on V07 proof of transaction-local context and pooled-connection isolation.

### 5.4 Unresolved owner decisions and implementation parameters — HIGH

The approved decisions do not supply: a complete action/resource/field/export permission catalog and grant matrix; Company Membership and Plant Assignment status vocabularies and all lifecycle transitions beyond D6's stated duplicate/history semantics; detailed invitation-edge choices; or P05 edge choices for additional business entities brought into scope. These are not silently resolved by the ERD, by D15, or by the fact that a proposed FK exists. Owner decisions must precede the affected implementation. P10 propagation bounds/cache TTLs and other session parameters remain values to select and test; they affect demonstrated authorization freshness but do not reverse the approved session architecture.

### 5.5 P09 documentation versus current manifest — MEDIUM

P09 closure and compatibility backlog prose still states exact runtime/package-manager versions are unselected, while the current manifest, lockfile, and `.nvmrc` contain concrete values. The validation register already records some V01/V03 evidence, but this documentation mismatch can cause an incorrect assumption about what is pinned or certified. Reconcile the P09 text and evidence references separately; a manifest pin is not proof that a new tenant compound relation, partial unique index, fallback, or authorization path works.

## 7. Recommended validation sequence

This sequence is a recommendation only. It does not update the execution register, authorize broad implementation, or change any decision.

1. **Reconcile artifacts and scope.** Preserve D1–D15 unchanged; mark D9–D13 as the operative X01–X05 policies in future doc reconciliation; resolve P05 tuple drift without expanding approvals. Confirm the concrete in-scope entities/aggregates and their P07 owner, scope, lifecycle, and transaction boundary.
2. **Establish the exact technical baseline.** Reconcile P09 documentation with the manifest/lockfile/runtime and existing V01/V03 evidence. On the pinned PostgreSQL/Prisma/provider set, validate canonical Better Auth User ID types and each approved X01–X05 DDL/Prisma shape. Check PostgreSQL-required target unique keys rather than assuming them. No tenant compatibility is established by prior general multi-schema validation.
3. **Validate structure and lifecycle constraints.** For X01–X05, inspect generated schema and SQL, `RESTRICT` actions, nullability, key order, target-key prerequisites, and migration history. For X02/X04/X05, test correct and wrong-parent cases; where the approved Core fallback is necessary, prove validation occurs in the same transaction and include the required negative tests. Validate D1/D6 candidate current-state guards without inventing D6 status predicates, and prove historical episodes remain insertable.
4. **Validate transitions and concurrency.** Test D1 slot occupancy; D2 verified-identity and atomic invitation redemption once its edges are decided; D3 restore/terminal/regrant/history behavior; D4 current-status semantics and audit-only timestamps; D5 non-singleton database cardinality and provisioning boundary; D6 active/current pair uniqueness and new historical episodes. Exercise races and fresh migration replay plus applicable upgrade from current migration history.
5. **Build the authorization proof only against owner-approved grants.** Resolve the in-scope permission/grant catalog and open status/lifecycle questions before implementing affected grants. Exercise actual protected handlers/services and owner-controlled data access: current session/account, membership chain, explicit company/plant scope, typed roles, deny-by-default, target ownership, query predicates, cache partitioning, and bounded revocation/change propagation. Test direct invocation and cross-scope negative cases, not just UI visibility.
6. **Address conditional database/security controls.** Decide explicitly whether any concrete tables use RLS. If selected, complete V07 pooling/session-context evidence first, then test missing-context denial, transaction cleanup/reuse, reads and writes, and actual least-privilege runtime roles. Validate V10 privilege boundaries and D15 Service Principal behavior if background/integration identities enter scope.
7. **Review the gate evidence.** Attach reproducible versions, provider/roles, migrations, tests, CI results, residual risks, and reviewer to the applicable register. Change V04 only after its full criteria pass; V05–V10 and Phase 1 remain independently gated.

## 8. Final recommendation and status boundary

**Proceed to staged technical validation** of the approved D1–D15 policies and P05 X01–X05, using the exact later-approved tuples and each decision's stated fallback. **Do not mark V04 implementation-ready or PASS.** The current repository has no tenant model or protected authorization boundary, and no technical validation for these tenant decisions has been run. Owner input is still required before implementing invitation edges, a complete permission/grant catalog, lifecycle/status details not decided by D1–D6, or any additional P05 edge that becomes in scope.

This review changes no source decision, schema, migration, application code, tests, CI, or gate status. No build, test, migration, database query, or technical validation was performed.
